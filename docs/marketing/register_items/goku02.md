## Description (English)

Son Goku figure from Dragon Ball Z (ドラゴンボールZ), part of the Grandista series by Banpresto.
Sealed, box in good condition with no notable damage.

This striking figure captures Goku in his Super Saiyan form, mid-battle with an intense fighting stance. His golden spiked hair and determined expression showcase the iconic transformation, while his orange gi and blue undershirt are faithfully recreated with dynamic sculpting detail. The figure conveys the raw power and energy of one of anime's most legendary warriors.

- Approximately 22cm tall
- Made by Banpresto (BANDAI SPIRITS)
- No visible damage on box
- Includes display base
