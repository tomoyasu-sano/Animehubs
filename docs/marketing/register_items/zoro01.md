## Description (English)

Roronoa Zoro figure from One Piece (ワンピース), New World arc version.
Opened, no box.

Zoro stands in a cool pose wearing a black checkered jacket open at the front, green haramaki sash, dark pants, and orange sunglasses. His iconic three swords are tucked at his waist. The chest scar from Mihawk is visible.

- Approximately 13cm tall
- Manufacturer unknown

- Clear blue display stand with support arm included
