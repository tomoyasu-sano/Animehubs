## Description (English)

Silvers Rayleigh figure from ONE PIECE (ワンピース), part of the King of Artist series by BANDAI SPIRITS.
Sealed, box has minor surface scratches.

The "Dark King" Rayleigh is depicted in his iconic casual appearance, standing confidently with his sword at his side. He wears an open orange shirt, dark patterned sarong-style pants, and a gray cloak draped over his shoulders. The figure captures Rayleigh's calm yet powerful presence as the former first mate of the Roger Pirates.

- Approximately 23cm tall
- Made by BANDAI SPIRITS
- Minor scratches on box exterior
- Includes display base
